Ask Senators Padilla and Schiff to vote for new Senate leadership

Senate Democrats are scheduled to choose their leader by secret ballot on Tuesday, December 1, and the vote may be moved earlier, so please call this week. Senator Chris Van Hollen of Maryland is challenging Chuck Schumer, and Indivisible has called on Senate Democrats to choose new leadership.

The ballot is secret, so a public statement is the only commitment we can use to verify.

Why new leadership? Compare the two records

Hi, I’m a constituent from Los Angeles. Please vote no on Chuck Schumer for Majority Leader and yes on Chris Van Hollen. Please also ask the Senator to state publicly who he plans to vote for, and please log my call. Thank you.
