Two calls before December 1

Ask Senators Padilla and Schiff to vote for new Senate leadership

Senate Democrats will choose their leader by secret ballot on Tuesday, December 1. Senator Chris Van Hollen of Maryland is challenging Chuck Schumer, who has led the caucus since 2017, and Indivisible has called on Senate Democrats to choose new leadership.

Because the ballot is secret, a public statement is the only commitment constituents can verify, which is why the script below asks for one.

Where our senators stand: Senator Padilla has not committed to either candidate. Senator Schiff has taken no public position. As of November 13, 2026. Source: Drop Site News whip count.

Say this

Hi, I’m a constituent from Los Angeles. Please vote no on Chuck Schumer for Majority Leader and yes on Chris Van Hollen, and please ask the Senator to state his position publicly. Please log my call. Thank you.
Calling from:

A staffer or a voicemail box will answer. Either one counts. The call takes about a minute.

Tap to call

  • Schiff, Los Angeles Best now Staffed weekdays during business hours (310) 914-7300
  • Schiff, Washington Best now Takes voicemail after hours (202) 224-3841
  • Padilla, Los Angeles Best now Takes voicemail after hours (310) 231-4494
  • Padilla, Washington Business hours only, no voicemail after hours (202) 224-3553

One call to each senator is enough. On a computer, dial the numbers shown.

Thank you.

One more step: tell us what you heard, either by replying to our email or by writing to hello@wehofairfaxindivisible.org, even if the staffer said only that the message would be passed along. We will report back to the list on what the two offices are saying.

Then send this page to one California friend.
