No Kings: Vote Early

📍 West Hollywood Park · 647 N. San Vicente Blvd. Saturday, October 17, 11:00 AM–1:00 PM

Bring your completed ballot. We will meet at West Hollywood Park for poster-making and a Q&A on how to vote, track, and return your ballot. At 1:00 PM we will march together to the official ballot drop box at West Hollywood City Hall and drop off our ballots. Bring friends and family.

This is our part of No Kings: Vote Early, a national day of action. Unlike earlier No Kings days, it is not purely a protest but a day of nonpartisan voter participation, built on the premise that we decide who represents us and that the best way to protect the right to vote is to exercise it.

Sign and seal your return envelope before you come. If you are not ready to vote yet, come anyway and we will help you make a plan. Please keep posters about voting rather than candidates or ballot measures, both because the event is nonpartisan and because state law bars campaign signs within 100 feet of a ballot drop box.

No Kings: Vote Early events are nonpartisan and are designed to increase participation from all eligible voters. A core principle behind all No Kings events is a commitment to nonviolent action. Weapons of any kind, including those legally permitted, should not be brought to events.

On the Horizon · General Election, November 3, 2026

The work that decides the House starts now

After Proposition 50 redrew the map, the 48th Congressional District now stretches from the San Diego suburbs into Palm Springs and Indian Wells, and its registration leans Democratic by about ten points. The June 2 primary set the matchup: Marni von Wilpert (D) against Jim Desmond (R). It is one of a handful of seats that could decide control of Congress, and we are hoping to put West Hollywood and Fairfax volunteers to work on it through the fall.
