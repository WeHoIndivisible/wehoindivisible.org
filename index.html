Senate Leadership Vote · December 1

Ask Senators Padilla and Schiff to back new leadership for Senate Democrats

Senate Democrats will choose their leader for the new majority by secret ballot on Tuesday, December 1, and Senator Chris Van Hollen of Maryland is challenging Chuck Schumer. The vote may be moved earlier, so please call this week. Neither of our senators has committed publicly, and a public statement before the vote is what we are asking for.

Our call page has a short script and one-tap buttons for both senators’ offices. The two calls take about two minutes. If you want the reasons first, compare the two records.

General Election Results · November 3, 2026

Both chambers of Congress change hands in January

On November 3, voters gave Democrats majorities in both the House and the Senate. In the Senate, Democrats flipped seven Republican-held seats. In the House, they flipped seats across the country, including California’s 48th District, the race we took on this fall. Marni von Wilpert is projected to win it by a wide margin over Jim Desmond, in a district that leaned Republican until Proposition 50 redrew it.

To everyone who wrote postcards to CA-48 voters, came to No Kings: Vote Early on October 17, or helped a neighbor make a plan to vote: thank you.

Closer to home, two races are still too close to call: Los Angeles mayor and the third open seat on the West Hollywood City Council. Los Angeles County keeps counting mail ballots for weeks after Election Day. Any ballot postmarked by November 3 that arrives within seven days will be counted, so expect the numbers below to change.
